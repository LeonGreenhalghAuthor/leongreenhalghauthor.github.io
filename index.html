Shardfall

Enter the world of Velora.

Power did not die. It shattered.

In the fractured land of Velora, divine magic survives within crystalline shards, the broken remnants of ancient gods. Each grants extraordinary power. Each demands a terrible price.

Nyssa is a runaway thief surviving beneath the watch of the Covenant, the oppressive regime that rules Velora. When she stumbles upon an ancient shard, she is transformed from a common criminal into one of the empire's most wanted fugitives.

Kyron has spent his life fighting for the rebellion. As the burden of leadership threatens to fall upon him, he must decide whether he can win a war without surrendering himself to the rage of the shard he wields.

Thalia wants only a quiet life, but the healing shard she bears ensures she can never truly have one. As the Covenant's reach closes in, she must choose between keeping its power hidden and using it to save those around her.

As ancient powers awaken and war engulfs Velora, these three Shardmancers will become entwined in a conflict that could reshape the world forever.

The Screaming Glass is the first novel in the Shardfall epic fantasy series, perfect for readers who enjoy layered world-building, morally complex characters, and magic systems where every gift comes with a cost.
